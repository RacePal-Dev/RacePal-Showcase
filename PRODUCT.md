# Product overview — public summary

RacePal is a pre-release karting performance-intelligence platform centered on auditable telemetry workflows. A driver, coach, or team can use validated session data to understand performance; future capabilities aim to combine comparisons, simulation, longitudinal evidence, and careful recommendations.

## Guiding principles

- Evidence before narrative; derived results must identify their provenance and limitations.
- Reproducible, deterministic calculations where applicable.
- Clear separation of local user experience and server-authoritative operations.
- Modular architecture so external formats, storage, UI, and algorithms evolve independently.
- Human oversight for consequential engineering and product decisions.

## Status language

- **Implemented:** source exists in the private implementation and has corresponding repository-level evidence.
- **Packaged / not deployed:** deployment material is present; live operation has not been established.
- **Planned:** accepted roadmap work, not delivered functionality.
- **Deferred:** intentionally postponed.
